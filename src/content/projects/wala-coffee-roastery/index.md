<div class="pinfo__text"><p><a href="https://walacoffee.ae/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">Wala Coffee &amp; Roastery</a> is a 450 sqm <strong>specialty coffee house and roastery in Al Quoz</strong>, Dubai – a bright, contemporary space defined by natural textures, warm timber, brass details and clean architectural lines. <strong>Ethiopian-inspired elements</strong> run through the interior as a quiet reference to the origins of coffee, from earthy tones to handcrafted objects.<br><br>The layout brings several functions under one roof: a specialty coffee bar, a retail display for beans and brewing equipment, a lounge, communal seating and the roastery itself. An open ceiling, feature lighting and integrated greenery keep the large volume welcoming, while custom joinery and bespoke furniture balance the needs of the baristas with a relaxed customer experience.<br><br>A warehouse unit of this size needs serious engineering behind the finishes. Crystal Fit built the project <strong>from shell &amp; core to handover</strong>: civil works, complete MEP and HVAC, power for the coffee and roasting equipment, drainage, lighting, glass and metal works, specialist finishes and all joinery. We worked from the client’s design and turned it into a buildable, approved project.<br><br>Approvals and inspections with <strong>Dubai Municipality, Dubai Civil Defence and DEWA</strong> ran in parallel with the works, followed by testing and commissioning of every system. The result is a destination where guests come for coffee, stay to work and leave with freshly roasted beans – one of our largest <strong>Food &amp; Beverage (F&amp;B) fit-outs</strong> in Dubai.<br><br>Before any material was ordered, finishes, timber tones and brass details were approved on physical samples with the client, and every dimension was verified on site against the drawings – the way we start every project.</p></div>
