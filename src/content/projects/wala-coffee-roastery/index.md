<div class="pinfo__text"><p><a href="https://walacoffee.ae/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">Wala Coffee &amp; Roastery</a> is a 450 sqm <strong>specialty coffee house and roastery in Al Quoz</strong>, Dubai, built inside a warehouse unit. One floor combines a coffee bar, a retail display for beans and brewing equipment, a lounge, communal seating and a working roastery.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> shell &amp; core fit-out and civil works; complete MEP and HVAC, including dedicated power, exhaust, fresh air and drainage for the coffee and roasting equipment; glass and metal works; all <strong>custom joinery</strong>; approvals with <strong>Dubai Municipality, Dubai Civil Defence and DEWA</strong>; project management through testing, commissioning and handover. The interior design was by the client’s designer – we developed it into shop drawings and a buildable, approved project.<br><br><strong>The challenge was the joinery.</strong> The feature walls are timber panels with a pixel-pattern perforation, where the pattern has to run continuously across panel joints, around wall lights and service access points. We set out every panel in shop drawings, produced them in our joinery workshop and dry-fitted them before installation, so that joints, holes and fixtures line up on site exactly as drawn. The same tolerance applied to the bar counters, brass details and display shelving.<br><br>The roastery itself needed separate engineering: equipment loads, exhaust routes and fire-safety measures were coordinated with Civil Defence requirements before installation, then tested and commissioned with the rest of the systems. Finishes, timber tones and brass were approved on physical samples with the client before production, and the unit was handed over ready to trade – one of our largest <strong>Food &amp; Beverage (F&amp;B) fit-outs</strong> in Dubai.</p></div>
