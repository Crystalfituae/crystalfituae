<div class="pinfo__text"><p>Interior design of <strong>West Gate Cinema</strong> in Nairobi, Kenya – six halls for around 700 guests in a well-known shopping mall.<br><br>Four different hall concepts vary seat comfort, materials and audio-visual systems. The main 194-seat hall features tilted purple acoustic panels with integrated LED lines and <strong>Dolby Atmos sound</strong> (28 speakers, 4 subwoofers). A colourful kids' cinema and a VIP hall with full-recline leather seats and personal tables serve the most important audiences.<br><br>Crystal Fit delivered <strong>design development, material selection, equipment layouts, shop drawings and acoustic calculations</strong> for every hall.</p></div>
