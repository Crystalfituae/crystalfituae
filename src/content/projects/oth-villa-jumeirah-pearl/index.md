<div class="pinfo__text"><p>A 650 sqm <strong>luxury villa on Pearl Jumeirah</strong>, Dubai: a double-height living room, a home cinema, a study, bedrooms and bathrooms.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> complete <strong>interior design</strong> – layouts, concept, lighting design, materials, joinery design, finishes and furniture specifications and 3D visualisation – with design-stage project management. Construction was carried out by the client’s contractor.<br><br><strong>The challenge was the home cinema and the marble feature wall.</strong> A home cinema is a technical room: it needs acoustic isolation from the rest of the house, sound absorption inside, a dark ceiling with the starry fibre-optic effect, hidden speakers, a projector or screen position and ventilation that stays silent. We designed the room with its acoustic wall and ceiling build-ups, seating levels and equipment positions, and coordinated them with the AV specialist’s requirements.<br><br>In the double-height living room, the book-matched marble feature wall had to read as one continuous image across its full height. We drew the slab layout with the veining matched, the joints aligned with the room’s proportions and the fixing method for the height, so the stone supplier and contractor could select and install the slabs exactly as designed. The study has integrated shelving, and the bathrooms combine oval backlit mirrors with fluted timber cabinetry. Every room was approved in 3D, and the specification package gave the client a precise basis for tendering and construction – an example of our <strong>villa interior design</strong> work in Dubai. Lighting scenes for every room were included in the package for the electrical contractor.</p></div>
