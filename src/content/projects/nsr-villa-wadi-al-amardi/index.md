<div class="pinfo__text"><p>A 620 sqm <strong>modern villa interior in Wadi Al Amardi</strong>, Dubai: living rooms opening to the garden, a master suite with a stone headboard wall, family rooms and marble bathrooms.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> complete <strong>interior design</strong> – space planning, concept, lighting design, materials, joinery and finishes specifications and 3D visualisation – with design-stage project management. Construction was carried out by the client’s contractor.<br><br><strong>The challenge was the large-format stone and the linear lighting.</strong> The interior relies on large-format stone, timber wall panelling and linear ceiling lights that run continuously from room to room. To keep the lines clean, we set out the stone module, panel joints and light lines on one grid for each room, aligned doors, switches and diffusers to that grid, and specified the stone sizes so that cuts fall where they are not visible.<br><br>The living rooms open fully to the garden, so floor levels, thresholds, drainage and the choice of indoor and outdoor finishes were designed together to make the transition seamless. The master bedroom is framed by a stone headboard wall with integrated lighting, and the bathrooms combine veined marble with bronze fittings, with the slab layout drawn for each wall. Every space was approved in 3D before the specifications were issued, so the client could tender and build the interior with a complete package of drawings, materials and quantities. The result is a calm, architectural interior – an example of our <strong>villa interior design</strong> work for modern family homes in Dubai. Lighting scenes were specified room by room.</p></div>
