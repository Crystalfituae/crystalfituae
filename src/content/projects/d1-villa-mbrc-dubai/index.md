<div class="pinfo__text"><p>A 450 sqm <strong>family villa in District One, Mohammed Bin Rashid City</strong>, finished in a light, elegant neo-classic style, with a private floor that includes a master suite and a nursery.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> interior design, technical and MEP drawings, community approvals, MEP modifications, wall mouldings, layered ceilings with cove lighting, polished marble floors, custom joinery, lighting, furnishing and project management – design and fit-out, through to handover.<br><br><strong>The challenge was neo-classic detailing at villa scale.</strong> Mouldings, layered ceilings and marble floors leave no room for improvisation: every moulding line has to meet the door frames, ceiling steps and light coves exactly. We set out the wall and ceiling mouldings room by room in shop drawings, aligned them with the doors, windows and joinery, and coordinated the cove lighting, AC diffusers and sprinklers inside the ceiling design so that no service breaks the classical lines.<br><br>The polished marble floors were laid out with the slabs selected and dry-laid for colour and veining before installation. The nursery and master suite were designed for comfort and easy maintenance: washable finishes, soft lighting scenes and storage built into the joinery. Furniture, rugs and crystal chandeliers were specified, supplied and installed by our team, so the client received a finished, furnished home rather than an empty shell. The work was done in an occupied community under its rules for access and working hours, and the villa was handed over ready to live in – an example of our <strong>villa design and fit-out</strong> work in Dubai.</p></div>
