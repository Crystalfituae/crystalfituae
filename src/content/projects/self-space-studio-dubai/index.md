<div class="pinfo__text"><p><a href="https://self.space/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">SELF.SPACE</a> is <strong>Dubai’s first AI-powered self-portrait studio</strong> – a 250 sqm industrial unit in Ghoshi Warehouse City, Al Quoz, with three professional photo studios, changing rooms, make-up tables, a lounge, reception and storage.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> interior design, technical and MEP drawings, approvals with <strong>Dubai Municipality, Dubai Civil Defence and DEWA</strong> handled in-house, MEP works, three cycloramas, polished concrete floors, metallic wall panels, shadow-gap skirting, frameless doors clad in pressed metal sheets, joinery and project management – design and build, through to handover.<br><br><strong>The challenge was a tight deadline with overseas sourcing.</strong> Part of the finishes and studio equipment came from abroad, and the opening date was fixed. We ordered the long-lead items on day one, ran approvals in parallel with the design and site works, and sequenced the trades so that cycloramas, floors and metal cladding were finished in time for the equipment installation – the studio was delivered on time.<br><br>Photo studios have their own technical rules: cycloramas need seamless curved surfaces with no visible joints, lighting rigs need dedicated power and fixing points in the ceiling, and the floor must stay flat and clean for shooting. The luxury industrial style was detailed with precision – shadow-gap skirting instead of trims, frameless doors in pressed metal and metallic panels aligned to one grid. The result turns a raw warehouse unit into a cosy, atmospheric creative space – a <strong>commercial fit-out</strong> for a new kind of business in Dubai. The client received as-built drawings and completion certificates with the keys.</p></div>
