<div class="pinfo__text"><p>Hanah is a 55 sqm <strong>luxury chocolate boutique</strong> in Al Barsha, Dubai, designed like a jewellery store. The brief was to present handmade chocolates and gift boxes as precious objects, and to give customers a comfortable place to sit, taste and choose.<br><br>Wave-shaped <strong>bronze-toned wall panels</strong> wrap the room, with illuminated niches for gift boxes set into the curves. A long glass display counter puts the chocolates at eye level, and a curved white lounge sofa invites guests to sit down for a tasting. Warm, concealed lighting brings out the textures of the panels and the packaging.<br><br>Chocolate needs stable conditions, so the display counter, lighting and air conditioning were planned together to keep the products cool without hot spots from the lamps. The curved panels and niches were detailed as <strong>bespoke joinery</strong>, made in the workshop and then installed and finished on site with precise joints.<br><br>Crystal Fit delivered the boutique as <strong>design and build</strong> – interior design, technical drawings, approvals, MEP, bespoke joinery, display counters, lighting and decorative finishes. It is a small retail space that shows how much character careful design and craftsmanship can add – and how a Food &amp; Beverage (F&amp;B) brand can be presented like a luxury label.<br><br>Every finish was approved on physical samples – the bronze tone of the panels, the stone of the counter and the fabric of the sofa – before production started. On site we verified each dimension so that the curves of the panels met the niches, the counter and the ceiling exactly, without visible gaps or filler.</p></div>
