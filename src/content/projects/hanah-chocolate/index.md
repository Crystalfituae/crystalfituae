<div class="pinfo__text"><p>Hanah is a 55 sqm <strong>luxury chocolate boutique in Al Barsha, Dubai</strong>, designed like a jewellery store: illuminated niches for gift boxes, a glass display counter and a lounge sofa for tastings.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> interior design, technical and MEP drawings, approvals, MEP and air conditioning, bespoke joinery, curved bronze-toned wall panels, display counters, concealed lighting, decorative finishes and project management to handover.<br><br><strong>The challenge was curved joinery with precise niches.</strong> The wave-shaped wall panels wrap the whole room, and the illuminated niches for the gift boxes are cut into the curves. Each panel was developed in shop drawings with its radius, niche positions and lighting channels, produced in the workshop and then installed and finished on site, so the curves run continuously and the joints and niches line up exactly as designed.<br><br>Chocolate needs stable conditions, so the display counter, lighting and air conditioning were planned together: LED lighting with low heat output, cooled display and supply air positioned to avoid hot spots on the products. The bronze tone of the panels, the stone of the counter and the fabric of the sofa were approved on physical samples before production, and every dimension was verified on site before the panels were made. The boutique shows how a small Food &amp; Beverage (F&amp;B) brand can be presented like a luxury label when design, joinery and engineering are delivered by one team. Air conditioning and display cooling were tested with the products in place before opening, and the client received as-built drawings with the handover.</p></div>
