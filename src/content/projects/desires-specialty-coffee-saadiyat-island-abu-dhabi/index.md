<div class="pinfo__text"><p>Desire’s Specialty Coffee is an 85 sqm <strong>specialty coffee shop on Saadiyat Island, Abu Dhabi</strong>: a full coffee bar, a pastry display and seating in a compact unit, with a sculpted folded-wall feature in white and terracotta.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> concept and technical design, MEP drawings, landlord and municipality approvals in Abu Dhabi, MEP works, custom joinery made in our workshop, the folded-wall feature, finishing works, coordination with the coffee equipment suppliers and project management to handover.<br><br><strong>The challenge was a small unit with no room for error.</strong> The bar had to fit the barista’s workflow, the espresso machine, grinders, fridges and pastry display into a few metres. Water, drainage, power and ventilation for every machine were coordinated with the equipment suppliers before the joinery was fabricated, so the machines fitted and worked from day one. The folded-wall feature was set out in shop drawings and built with the lighting integrated, so the sculpted surfaces read cleanly without visible fixings.<br><br>Working in Abu Dhabi meant a different set of landlord and municipality requirements from Dubai. Our team prepared the drawings and submissions for the local authorities, verified the unit on site before fabrication and installed the joinery made in our workshop in a short programme. Finishes were chosen to stay clean and hard-wearing under daily use. The project shows that our <strong>Food &amp; Beverage (F&amp;B) fit-out</strong> team delivers across the UAE, not only in Dubai. All equipment was tested with the suppliers on site before the opening, and the client received as-built drawings with the handover.</p></div>
