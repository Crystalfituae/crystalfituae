<div class="pinfo__text"><p>An 85 sqm <strong>specialty coffee shop on Saadiyat Island</strong>, Abu Dhabi, with a soft, gallery-like interior in white and terracotta. Saadiyat is the cultural district of the capital, home to museums and galleries, and the cafe borrows that calm, curated character.<br><br>A sculpted <strong>folded-wall feature</strong> gives the small space a strong identity, while warm timber tables, a custom-coloured espresso machine and carefully placed lighting keep the attention on the coffee. The layout fits a full coffee bar, a pastry display and comfortable seating into a compact footprint without feeling crowded.<br><br>Small units leave no room for error. Every centimetre of the bar was planned around the barista’s workflow, the equipment and the services behind it: water, drainage, power and ventilation were coordinated with the coffee equipment suppliers before fabrication, so the machines fitted and worked from day one. Finishes were chosen to stay clean and hard-wearing under daily use.<br><br>Crystal Fit delivered the cafe as <strong>design and build</strong> – concept and technical design, approvals, MEP, custom joinery, finishes and coordination with the coffee equipment suppliers – showing that our <strong>Food &amp; Beverage (F&amp;B) fit-out</strong> team works across the UAE, not only in Dubai.<br><br>Working in Abu Dhabi meant a different set of landlord and municipality requirements from Dubai. Our team prepared the drawings and submissions for the local authorities, verified the unit on site before fabrication and installed the joinery made in our workshop, so the client dealt with one contractor from the first sketch to the opening day.</p></div>
