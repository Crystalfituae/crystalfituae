<div class="pinfo__text"><p>Architectural design of a 540 sqm <strong>contemporary villa in Dubai Hills Estate</strong>, with travertine facades, timber louvres, cantilevered balconies and a pool terrace.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> <strong>architectural concept</strong> – massing, plans, facades, materials, facade lighting and 3D visualisation – with design-stage project management. Construction was carried out by the client’s contractor.<br><br><strong>The challenge was the deep cantilevered balconies.</strong> The facades are built around horizontal travertine bands and balconies that project far beyond the walls below. Deep cantilevers give shade and a strong character, but they need the structure, drainage and cladding resolved together. We set the cantilever depths with the structural logic in mind, kept drainage and waterproofing within the slab build-up, and detailed the travertine cladding on the soffits and edges so that the stone bands read as continuous lines.<br><br>Dark timber louvres control sunlight and privacy on the upper floor without closing the views, and large glazing on the ground floor opens the living areas to the pool terrace and garden, shaded by the balconies above. Integrated facade lighting was designed as part of the architecture – hidden in the soffits and louvres – so that the house is equally striking at night without visible fittings. The concept respects the Dubai Hills community guidelines for heights, setbacks and materials. Every elevation and the garden were presented to the client in 3D before the design was frozen – an example of our <strong>architectural design</strong> work for private villas in Dubai. The package was handed to the client for permit drawings and construction.</p></div>
