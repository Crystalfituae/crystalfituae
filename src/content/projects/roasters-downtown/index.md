<div class="pinfo__text"><p>A 234 sqm <a href="https://www.instagram.com/roasterscoffee_dxb/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">Roasters</a> <strong>specialty coffee house on The Boulevard, Downtown Dubai</strong>, with views towards the Old Town. On one of the busiest streets in the city, the cafe had to feel open and inviting from the outside while staying calm and comfortable inside.<br><br>Full-height glazing, lush planting and a light, airy palette blur the line between the interior and the terrace. Green marble-top tables, soft upholstered chairs, a VIP seating area and a <strong>live-edge communal table</strong> create different moods – for a quick espresso, a working morning or a long meeting – while the coffee bar remains the visual focus of the room.<br><br>Downtown is managed by Emaar, so every detail – from the shopfront to the signage and the MEP routes – went through landlord review. Crystal Fit prepared the drawings and obtained approvals from <strong>Emaar, Dubai Municipality, Dubai Civil Defence and DEWA</strong>, coordinating the inspections in parallel with the works.<br><br>We delivered the project as <strong>design and build</strong>: design development, technical drawings, approvals, MEP and HVAC, kitchen and bar coordination, bespoke joinery and finishing works, through to handover with completion certificates. The result is a bright, green cafe that works for residents and visitors throughout the day, from the first coffee to the evening crowd.<br><br>The planting, glazing and lighting were treated as one system: daylight from the glass front, warm artificial light for the evening and greenery placed where it is easy to maintain. Finishes and upholstery were approved on samples before ordering, so the final room matches the design boards that the brand signed off.</p></div>
