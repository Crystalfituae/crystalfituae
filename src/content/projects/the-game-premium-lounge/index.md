<div class="pinfo__text"><p>A 240 sqm <strong>premium gaming lounge in JBR</strong>, Dubai: rows of gaming PCs, private gaming rooms and a cinema lounge with recliner seating, a fibre-optic starry ceiling and RGB cove lighting.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> interior design, technical and MEP drawings, approvals with <strong>Dubai Municipality, Dubai Civil Defence and DEWA</strong>, power and data infrastructure, HVAC, acoustic treatment, the starry ceiling and lighting, joinery, finishing works and project management – <strong>turnkey</strong>, through to handover.<br><br><strong>The challenge was power, heat and sound.</strong> Dozens of high-end gaming PCs and screens running at the same time create heavy electrical loads, a lot of heat and a dense data network. We calculated the loads, arranged dedicated circuits for the gaming rows, sized the air conditioning for the equipment heat as well as the guests, and ran the data cabling in hidden routes to every station. Private rooms and the cinema lounge were acoustically separated from the main hall with dedicated wall and ceiling build-ups.<br><br>The immersive look is built on top of this technical layer: dark acoustic surfaces absorb sound, the fibre-optic starry ceiling and RGB cove lighting are wired on separate controls, and joinery for the gaming desks hides cables and gives access for maintenance. Approvals with the authorities ran in parallel with the works, and every system was tested under full load before handover. The result is a lounge that works as well at 2 a.m. on a full night as on the opening day – an example of our technical <strong>commercial fit-out</strong> work.</p></div>
