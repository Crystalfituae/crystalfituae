<div class="pinfo__text"><p><a href="https://www.instagram.com/narrowcoffee/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">Narrow Specialty Coffee</a> is a 35 sqm <strong>specialty coffee shop in Sharjah</strong> that proves a small space can have a strong identity. True to its name, the unit is narrow, so the design had to work with every centimetre.<br><br>A grey microcement bar, navy blue retail shelving for coffee beans, built-in banquette seating and soft concealed lighting keep the focus on the coffee and the barista. The palette is restrained, letting the colour of the shelving and the products do the talking.<br><br>In a unit this size, the bar, storage and seating were planned as one piece of joinery. Built-in banquettes save space compared with loose furniture, the shelving doubles as retail display and storage, and the equipment was placed so the barista can work efficiently without crossing the customers’ path. Concealed lighting in the shelving and under the bar makes the narrow room feel wider and warmer.<br><br>Crystal Fit delivered the project as <strong>design and build</strong> – interior design, the microcement bar counter, bespoke joinery, lighting and finishing works. The result is a small <strong>Food &amp; Beverage (F&amp;B)</strong> space with a clear, memorable character – proof that a strong concept does not need a big footprint, only careful planning and well-made joinery.<br><br>The project followed our usual sequence: agreement on the scope and BOQ, approval of finishes on samples and an on-site survey before any joinery was produced, so every piece fitted the narrow unit first time. The microcement bar was finished by hand on site, giving the counter a seamless surface without joints that is easy to keep clean during a busy day.</p></div>
