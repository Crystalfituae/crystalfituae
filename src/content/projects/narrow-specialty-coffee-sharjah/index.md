<div class="pinfo__text"><p><a href="https://www.instagram.com/narrowcoffee/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">Narrow Specialty Coffee</a> is a 35 sqm <strong>specialty coffee shop in Al Layyeh, Sharjah</strong>: a narrow unit with a grey microcement bar, navy blue retail shelving for coffee beans and built-in banquette seating.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> interior design, the hand-finished microcement bar counter, bespoke joinery for shelving, storage and banquettes, concealed lighting, finishing works and project management to handover.<br><br><strong>The challenge was the width of the unit.</strong> In a space this narrow, the bar, storage, retail display and seating had to be planned as one piece of joinery, and the barista needed room to work without crossing the customers’ path. Built-in banquettes save space compared with loose furniture, the shelving doubles as retail display and storage, and the equipment was placed along one side so service and seating never collide. Concealed lighting in the shelving and under the bar makes the narrow room feel wider.<br><br>The project followed our usual sequence: agreement on the scope and BOQ, approval of finishes on samples and an on-site survey before any joinery was produced, so every piece fitted the unit first time with no site adjustments. The microcement bar was finished by hand on site, giving the counter a seamless surface without joints. The result is a small <strong>Food &amp; Beverage (F&amp;B) fit-out</strong> with a clear, memorable character – proof that a strong concept does not need a big footprint, only careful planning and well-made joinery. All equipment was installed and tested before handover, and the shop opened on the planned date.</p></div>
