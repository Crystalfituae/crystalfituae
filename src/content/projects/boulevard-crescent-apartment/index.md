<div class="pinfo__text"><p>A 100 sqm <strong>apartment in Boulevard Crescent Tower 1, Downtown Dubai</strong>, with a terrace overlooking the Boulevard, reworked into a calm, tactile family home.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> interior design, technical and MEP drawings, building management approvals, MEP modifications, the hand-sculpted plaster relief wall with concealed linear lighting, a refreshed kitchen and bathrooms, bespoke wardrobes and joinery, decorative finishes, lighting and project management – design and fit-out, through to handover.<br><br><strong>The challenge was the sculpted relief wall.</strong> The plaster relief in the dining area is made by hand on site, so there is no factory tolerance to rely on. We prepared the substrate, set out the relief pattern on the wall with the artisans, integrated the concealed linear lighting into the wall build-up before sculpting, and tested the light at night so that the shadows fall exactly as designed.<br><br>A tower apartment brings its own rules: building management approvals for every change, works only in permitted hours, protection of lifts and corridors and limited delivery slots. MEP changes in the kitchen and bathrooms were coordinated with the building’s risers and approved before the works. The bespoke wardrobes and joinery were made in the workshop to dimensions verified on site and installed in a short programme. Warm neutral tones and natural textures make the compact layout feel generous and cohesive – a typical <strong>apartment renovation</strong> by our team in Downtown Dubai, where the client deals with one team from design to handover. The apartment was handed over ready to move in, with all building management approvals closed.</p></div>
