<div class="pinfo__text"><p>Architectural design of a 420 sqm <strong>contemporary villa in Dubai Hills Estate</strong>, with a pool garden and a sunken lounge. The interior of the same villa is shown as a separate project.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> <strong>architectural concept</strong> – massing, plans, facades, materials, landscape concept and 3D visualisation – with design-stage project management. Construction was carried out by the client’s contractor.<br><br><strong>The challenge was shading a glass house in Dubai’s climate.</strong> The client wanted full-height glazing towards the garden, but large glass walls facing the sun make a house hot and expensive to cool. We used the building’s volumes to shade itself: deep overhangs and timber soffits protect the glazing from high sun, the solid stone and brick volumes face the hottest orientation, and the openings face the garden and the pool, where views and shade work together.<br><br>The garden side features a sunken lounge set next to an infinity pool, so that seated guests have their eyes at water level. We worked out the levels, retaining walls, waterproofing and drainage of the sunken area at concept stage, so it could be built without surprises. Grey stone, dark brick and warm timber soffits combine in bold, simple volumes that suit the Dubai Hills community guidelines. Interior and architecture were designed together, so the window positions, ceiling heights and structure supported the interior from the start. The concept was presented in 3D for every elevation and the garden before it was frozen – an example of our <strong>architectural design</strong> work for private villas.</p></div>
