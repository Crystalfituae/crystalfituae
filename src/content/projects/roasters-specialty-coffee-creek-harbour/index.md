<div class="pinfo__text"><p>A 265 sqm <a href="https://www.instagram.com/roasterscoffee_dxb/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">Roasters</a> <strong>waterfront cafe in Dubai Creek Harbour</strong>, within the Emaar community: indoor hall, coffee bar and an outdoor terrace on the promenade facing the Creek and the Downtown skyline.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> interior design, technical and MEP drawings, approvals with <strong>Emaar, Dubai Municipality, Dubai Civil Defence and DEWA</strong>, MEP and HVAC, terrace works, stone cladding, custom joinery, kitchen and bar equipment coordination with the brand’s suppliers, finishing works and project management to handover with completion certificates.<br><br><strong>The challenge was the stone bar and the terrace.</strong> The bar is clad in textured stone with bronze lettering, so every slab had to be templated on site, cut to the bar joinery and fixed with joints that line up with the lettering. The terrace needed its own engineering: outdoor-grade finishes, drainage for the open area, evening lighting and furniture, all detailed to Emaar’s promenade guidelines and approved before work started.<br><br>Emaar runs detailed design reviews before any site activity, so submissions, comments and inspections were planned into the programme from day one rather than added at the end. Exposed timber rafters, large openings to the terrace and services in the ceiling were coordinated on one set of drawings, so the hall stays clean and open. Finishes were approved on physical samples and the unit was verified on site before fabrication. The cafe was handed over ready to operate – one of many Roasters locations designed and built by our team across Dubai. Completion certificates and as-built drawings were handed over together with the keys.</p></div>
