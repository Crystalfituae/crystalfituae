<div class="pinfo__text"><p>A 265 sqm <a href="https://www.instagram.com/roasterscoffee_dxb/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">Roasters</a> <strong>waterfront cafe in Dubai Creek Harbour</strong>, with a terrace facing the Creek and the Downtown skyline. The cafe serves residents and visitors along the promenade from morning coffee to the evening, so the space had to work equally well for a quick takeaway and a long sit-down visit.<br><br>The centrepiece is a <strong>bar clad in textured stone</strong> with bronze lettering, set against a light, calm interior with exposed timber rafters, soft upholstered seating and greenery. Large openings connect the hall to the outdoor terrace, which extends the cafe towards the water and adds outdoor seating for the cooler months.<br><br>Working inside an Emaar community means detailed landlord design reviews before any work starts. We prepared the drawings and submissions for <strong>Emaar, Dubai Municipality, Dubai Civil Defence and DEWA</strong>, coordinated the kitchen and bar equipment with the brand’s suppliers and planned every inspection into the programme.<br><br>Crystal Fit delivered the cafe as <strong>design and build</strong> – design development, technical drawings, approvals, MEP and HVAC, terrace works, custom joinery, stone cladding and finishing – and handed over a ready-to-operate cafe with all completion certificates. It is one of many Roasters locations we have designed and built across Dubai.<br><br>The terrace needed its own details: outdoor-grade finishes and furniture, lighting for the evening and drainage for the open area, all designed to Emaar’s promenade guidelines. Inside, the stone bar was templated on site and fabricated to fit the counter equipment exactly, so the baristas have a smooth, efficient workflow during the busiest hours of the day.</p></div>
