<div class="pinfo__text"><p><strong><a href="https://www.instagram.com/gasteria.dubai/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">Gasteria</a></strong> is a 280 sqm all-day dining restaurant in BLVD Heights, Downtown Dubai: neo-classic interior with ceiling mouldings, book-matched natural stone, timber floors, a <strong>4.5 m feature bottle display</strong>, an open show kitchen with pizza oven and a front-of-house bar.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> BOQ and technical package, technical drawings, Emaar and authority approvals, review of contractors’ shop drawings, material approvals, technical supervision and <strong>project management</strong> through to handover. The concept design was by the client’s designer; civil, kitchen, MEP, stone and joinery works were carried out by separate contractors appointed by the client.<br><br><strong>The challenge was project management.</strong> <strong>More than 15 teams</strong> – the concept designer, contractors for every trade and overseas suppliers of long-lead custom items – had to work under one roof, to one schedule. Shell &amp; core fit-out ran together with a facade renovation, and the site went through <strong>51 internal Emaar inspections</strong>. We ran one master programme for every team, held coordination meetings on site, sequenced the trades so that stone, mouldings, joinery and lighting met at the junctions shown on the drawings, and tracked every overseas order against the installation dates.<br><br>Every installed element was checked against the approved drawings and samples before the next trade could cover it, and every Emaar inspection was prepared and attended by our team. Gasteria shows how we work as the owner’s <strong>project manager</strong> when the construction is split between other contractors: one accountable team for programme, approvals and quality, and a restaurant that opened with the neo-classic design complete in every detail.</p></div>
