<div class="pinfo__text"><p>A 222 sqm <a href="https://www.instagram.com/yoy_dubai/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">YOY</a> <strong>Ukrainian fire-cooking restaurant at The Pointe</strong>, Palm Jumeirah. Built around an open kitchen with wood-fired cooking, the restaurant brings the warmth of Ukrainian home cooking to one of Dubai’s busiest waterfront destinations.<br><br>The interior celebrates natural, rustic materials: raw timber tables and benches, woven textiles, ceramic vases and <strong>dramatic dried-grass ceiling installations</strong> above the dining hall. The open kitchen is the theatrical heart of the room – guests see the fire, the chefs and the food being prepared.<br><br>Fire cooking is demanding to build. The kitchen needed <strong>exhaust, fresh air and fire suppression</strong> designed and installed to Civil Defence requirements, and the natural ceiling decor had to meet fire-safety rules. We coordinated the kitchen equipment, the MEP and the decor so that the rustic look and the safety systems work together without visible compromise.<br><br>Crystal Fit delivered the <strong>fit-out</strong> to the client’s design under Nakheel requirements – approvals with <strong>Nakheel, Dubai Municipality, Dubai Civil Defence and DEWA</strong>, kitchen MEP, joinery and finishing works – and handed over a restaurant ready for service. The project was the start of our work with YOY, which continued with the YOY Neo Bistro at Shangri-La.<br><br>The Pointe is a waterfront destination managed by Nakheel, with its own design guidelines for shopfronts, signage and outdoor seating. We prepared the landlord submissions, coordinated the kitchen equipment supplier and verified every service point on site before installation, so the open kitchen worked from the first service and passed the inspections without rework.</p></div>
