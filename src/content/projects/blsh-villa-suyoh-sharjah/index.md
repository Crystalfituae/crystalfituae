<div class="pinfo__text"><p>A 480 sqm <strong>family villa in Al Suyoh, Sharjah</strong>: a formal entrance hall, a family living room, dining areas, bedrooms and bathrooms, mixing classic and contemporary interiors.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> complete <strong>interior design</strong> – layouts, concept, 3D visualisation, lighting, joinery and finishes specification – with design-stage project management. Construction was carried out by the client’s contractor.<br><br><strong>The challenge was combining two styles in one house.</strong> The family wanted a formal, classic welcome for guests and a bright, relaxed space for everyday life. We separated the two by function: the entrance hall and formal areas use walnut wall panels, a geometric marble inlay in the floor and a sculptural chandelier, while the family living room opens up with a light decorative ceiling and soft furniture. Transitions between the zones were designed with matching floor levels, door heights and lighting, so the house still reads as one home.<br><br>The marble inlay was drawn to scale with every piece, colour and joint, so it could be cut and laid exactly as designed, and the walnut panelling was detailed with its joints aligned to the doors and the chandelier. Bathrooms feature brass pendants, mosaics and arabesque details for a subtle oriental touch, specified with the client’s preferences for privacy and easy cleaning. Every room was approved in 3D, and the specification package listed all finishes and fittings – an early example of our <strong>villa interior design</strong> work in the UAE, from the years when we operated as a design bureau. Lighting scenes were specified room by room for the electrical contractor.</p></div>
