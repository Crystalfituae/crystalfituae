<div class="pinfo__text"><p>A 240 sqm <a href="https://www.instagram.com/roasterscoffee_dxb/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">Roasters</a> <strong>destination cafe in Nad Al Sheba Mall</strong>, a community mall developed by Dubai Holding. The cafe serves the surrounding residential neighbourhoods, so it was designed as a comfortable place to spend time, not just a coffee stop.<br><br>The interior combines warm wood flooring, a <strong>natural slate stone bar</strong> and bespoke decorative wall panels for a cosy yet bold atmosphere. Different seating areas – from lounge chairs to tables for families and groups – give guests a choice of how to spend their visit.<br><br>The project went through an extensive approval process and rigorous landlord inspections. Careful coordination with Dubai Holding met every requirement – from fire safety to MEP and finishes – without compromising the design. Submissions, comments and inspections were planned into the programme from the start, so approvals never held up the works on site.<br><br>Crystal Fit delivered the cafe <strong>turnkey</strong> – design development, technical drawings, approvals, procurement, MEP, bespoke joinery, construction and opening support. One team was responsible from the first drawing to the first coffee served, which is how we work with Roasters on every location.<br><br>Before works began, the BOQ and agreement fixed the full scope, the slate, timber and wall-panel finishes were signed off on samples, and the unit was surveyed on site. Every design decision was then checked against Dubai Holding’s fit-out guide, which is why the cafe passed its inspections and opened without redesign. The bespoke wall panels were produced in our workshop and installed after the services were complete, keeping the finished surfaces clean.</p></div>
