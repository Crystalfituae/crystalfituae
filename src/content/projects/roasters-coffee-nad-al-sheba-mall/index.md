<div class="pinfo__text"><p>A 240 sqm <a href="https://www.instagram.com/roasterscoffee_dxb/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">Roasters</a> <strong>destination cafe in Nad Al Sheba Mall</strong>, a community mall developed by Dubai Holding: slate stone bar, timber flooring, bespoke decorative wall panels and seating zones for families and groups.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> interior design, technical and MEP drawings, <strong>Dubai Holding</strong> and authority approvals, procurement, MEP works, bespoke joinery and wall panels, stone bar, construction and finishing works, project management and opening support – <strong>turnkey</strong>, under one contract.<br><br><strong>The challenge was the landlord approval process.</strong> Dubai Holding runs an extensive review and rigorous inspections, from fire safety to MEP and finishes. We checked every design decision against Dubai Holding’s fit-out guide before submission, built submissions, comment responses and inspections into the programme from day one, and kept approvals running in parallel with the works so they never held up the site. The cafe passed its inspections and opened without redesign.<br><br>Before works began, the BOQ and agreement fixed the full scope, the slate, timber and wall-panel finishes were signed off on samples, and the unit was surveyed on site. The bespoke wall panels were produced in the workshop to the surveyed dimensions and installed with the lighting integrated. One team was responsible from the first drawing to the first coffee served, which is how we work with Roasters on every location – a <strong>Food &amp; Beverage (F&amp;B) fit-out</strong> delivered within a demanding landlord system. Testing, commissioning and the final landlord inspection were completed before handover, and the client received the completion certificates with the keys.</p></div>
