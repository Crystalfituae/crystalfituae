<div class="pinfo__text"><p>A 360 sqm <a href="https://www.instagram.com/roasterscoffee_dxb/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">Roasters</a> <strong>specialty coffee house in Dubai Hills</strong>, within the Emaar community. One of the largest cafes in the Roasters family, it was designed for residents of the area who come for breakfast, work and weekend gatherings.<br><br>Terrazzo flooring, frameless doors, high-gloss veneer panels and high-grade stainless steel create a crisp, contemporary space. A large illuminated ring installation and green velvet armchairs add a bold touch to the otherwise clean palette, and the open layout keeps the bar visible from every seat.<br><br>All works were completed <strong>within the lease-free period</strong>, allowing the client to open on schedule. To make that possible, design, approvals and long-lead orders ran in parallel: drawings were submitted to Emaar while materials were being ordered, and site works started as soon as the permits were issued.<br><br>Crystal Fit delivered the cafe <strong>turnkey</strong> – design, drawings, Emaar and authority approvals, procurement, MEP, joinery, construction and opening support, all under one roof. For the client this meant one contract, one programme and one team accountable for the result – the approach we bring to every <strong>Food &amp; Beverage (F&amp;B) fit-out</strong>.<br><br>The sequence was the same as on every Roasters project: a detailed BOQ and one agreement, approval of terrazzo, veneer and steel finishes on samples, an on-site survey of the unit, then approvals and works in parallel. Keeping to that sequence is what made it possible to finish within the lease-free period and hand over a cafe ready to trade. The client signed one contract and dealt with one team.</p></div>
