<div class="pinfo__text"><p>A 360 sqm <a href="https://www.instagram.com/roasterscoffee_dxb/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">Roasters</a> <strong>specialty coffee house in Dubai Hills</strong>, within the Emaar community – one of the largest cafes in the Roasters family, with a coffee bar, indoor seating and a work-friendly layout for residents of the area.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> interior design and 3D visuals, technical and MEP drawings, <strong>Emaar and authority approvals</strong>, procurement, civil and fit-out works, MEP installation, custom joinery, terrazzo flooring, stainless steel and glass works, and project management to handover – <strong>turnkey</strong>, under one contract.<br><br><strong>The challenge was to build our own design exactly as in the 3D.</strong> The concept, created by our design team, combines terrazzo flooring, frameless doors, high-gloss veneer panels, high-grade stainless steel and a large illuminated ring installation over the seating. Each of these elements was developed into shop drawings with set-out, fixing and lighting details, and finishes were approved on physical samples against the 3D visuals before production. On site, the ring installation, its structural fixing and power were coordinated with the ceiling and MEP layout, so that it hangs, lights and aligns exactly as shown to the client at the concept stage.<br><br>All works were completed <strong>within the lease-free period</strong>. To make that possible, design, approvals and long-lead orders ran in parallel: drawings went to Emaar while materials were being ordered, and site works started as soon as the permits were issued. The client signed one contract, dealt with one team and opened on schedule – the approach we bring to every <strong>Food &amp; Beverage (F&amp;B) fit-out</strong>.</p></div>
