<div class="pinfo__text"><p>A 180 sqm <strong>apartment in Executive Towers, Business Bay</strong>, redesigned in a warm contemporary style: open kitchen and living room, bedrooms with upholstered headboard walls and new bathrooms.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> interior design, technical and MEP drawings, building management approvals, MEP modifications, a new open kitchen with island, full-height upholstered headboard walls, bathrooms with floating vanities, timber flooring, custom joinery, lighting, finishing works and project management – design and fit-out, through to handover.<br><br><strong>The challenge was opening the kitchen in an existing tower apartment.</strong> Turning a closed kitchen into an open one with an island means moving water, drainage, power and extraction in a building where risers and slab levels cannot change. We surveyed the existing services, planned the new routes within the floor and ceiling build-ups, obtained building management approval, and installed the island with its drainage and power before the timber floor was laid.<br><br>In the bedrooms, the full-height upholstered headboard walls were made as panels in the workshop and fixed on site with integrated reading lights and bedside power. Bathrooms were stripped back, re-waterproofed and fitted with floating vanities, whose wall fixings and concealed drainage were set out before tiling. Walnut-tone cabinetry, a white island and black stone worktops give the kitchen its character. Works followed the tower’s rules on noise, access and deliveries, and the apartment was handed over ready to move in – an example of our <strong>apartment renovation</strong> work in Business Bay. Final inspection by the building management was closed without comments.</p></div>
