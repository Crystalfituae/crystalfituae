<div class="pinfo__text"><p>Interior design of a 420 sqm <strong>contemporary villa in Dubai Hills Estate</strong>, with a double-height living room, an indoor bonsai garden under the staircase, bedrooms and dark stone bathrooms. The architecture of the same villa is shown as a separate project.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> complete <strong>interior design</strong> – layouts, concept, lighting design, materials and finishes, furniture specification and 3D visualisation – with design-stage project management. Construction was carried out by the client’s contractor.<br><br><strong>The challenge was the floating staircase above the indoor garden.</strong> The staircase rises over a bonsai garden inside the living room, so it had to look weightless and leave the garden open below. We designed it with a concealed steel structure fixed to the wall, timber treads without visible supports and a lighting plan for the garden underneath, and coordinated drainage, waterproofing and irrigation for the planting with the floor finishes around it.<br><br>The double-height living room is defined by a stone wall with backlit onyx inserts: we detailed the stone module, the onyx positions and the access to the lighting behind it, so the lamps can be serviced without removing the stone. Bronze details, dark stone bathrooms with sculptural mirrors and soft textiles complete the warm, luxurious interior. Interior and architecture were designed together, so window positions, ceiling heights and structure supported the interior concept from the start. Every room was approved in 3D before the specifications were issued – an example of our <strong>villa interior design</strong> work in Dubai. The specification package gave the contractor a complete basis for pricing and construction.</p></div>
