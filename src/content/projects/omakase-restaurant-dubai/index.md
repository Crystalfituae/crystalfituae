<div class="pinfo__text"><p><strong>Interior design concept</strong> for a Japanese omakase restaurant in Dubai, shown here in 3D visualisation.<br><br>The room is built around a <strong>curved red chef's counter</strong> where guests watch every course being prepared. A sculpted red ceiling with linear light lines follows the counter from above, while charcoal walls, a red patterned carpet, paper lanterns and a large art wall keep the space intimate and theatrical. A separate lounge and bar area extends the evening beyond the counter.<br><br>Crystal Fit developed the <strong>interior concept, layout and 3D visualisation</strong>.</p></div>
