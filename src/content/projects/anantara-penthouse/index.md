<div class="pinfo__text"><p>A 706 sqm <strong>luxury penthouse at Anantara The Palm</strong>, Palm Jumeirah: four bedrooms, a maid’s room, a gym, an office, a panoramic balcony and a private pool – taken from shell &amp; core to turnkey handover.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> interior design, technical and MEP drawings, building management and authority approvals, structural modifications, blockwork and insulation, MEP and sanitary installations, pool refurbishment and waterproofing, premium large-format tiling, a backlit onyx feature wall, the hand-painted spiral staircase, bespoke joinery, finishing works and project management – <strong>turnkey</strong>, through to handover.<br><br><strong>The challenge was structural work at the top of a hotel residence.</strong> The client wanted a panoramic balcony where the building had a sloped roof. We prepared the structural design and submissions, obtained full approvals from the building management and the authorities, and converted the sloped roof into a usable balcony with new waterproofing and drainage. The private pool was stripped back, re-waterproofed and re-tiled in Bali stone, and tested for leaks before the finishes went on.<br><br>Inside, the large-format stone and tiles needed flat substrates and precise setting-out, so blockwork, insulation and MEP were completed and checked before any finishes started. The onyx wall was set out slab by slab with its backlighting, and the spiral staircase was finished by hand on site. Works in an operating residence followed strict rules on access, noise and deliveries. The penthouse was handed over as a complete, customised home – an example of our in-house design and execution at the top end of <strong>residential fit-out</strong> in Dubai.</p></div>
