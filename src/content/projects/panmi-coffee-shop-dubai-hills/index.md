<div class="pinfo__text"><p><a href="https://www.instagram.com/panmi.ae/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">Panmi</a> is a 210 sqm <strong>bakery-cafe in Lime Garden, Dubai Hills</strong>, within the Emaar community: a full commercial kitchen and bakery, a coffee bar and a dining hall with arches, curved banquettes, terrazzo and sculptural lighting.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> shell &amp; core fit-out, technical and MEP drawings, approvals with <strong>Emaar, DDA and DEWA</strong>, complete MEP including LPG, kitchen exhaust and fresh air, joinery for arched niches and banquettes, terrazzo surfaces, finishing works, testing, commissioning and project management to handover. The interior design was by the client’s designer.<br><br><strong>The challenge was the kitchen and bakery engineering.</strong> Every oven, mixer, fryer and fridge needed power, water, drainage, gas, exhaust and fresh air, coordinated with the kitchen consultant and the design. The LPG installation and kitchen ventilation went through separate approvals and inspections. Before installation, the kitchen layout was verified on site against the equipment list, so every connection was in the right place when the equipment arrived and testing passed first time.<br><br>In front of house, the arched niches, curved banquettes and terrazzo surfaces were made and finished to the exact lines of the design, with finishes, fabrics and the terrazzo mix approved on samples before production. Our team prepared the submissions, answered every authority comment and coordinated testing and commissioning. The result is a bakery-cafe that is as efficient behind the counter as it is calm in front of it – a complete <strong>Food &amp; Beverage (F&amp;B) fit-out</strong> from shell &amp; core. The client received as-built drawings and completion certificates with the keys.</p></div>
