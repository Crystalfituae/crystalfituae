<div class="pinfo__text"><p><a href="https://www.instagram.com/panmi.ae/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">Panmi</a> is a 210 sqm <strong>Asian-inspired cafe</strong> in Lime Garden, Dubai Hills, with warm natural tones, terrazzo, sculptural lighting and custom furniture. Soft arches, curved sofas and paper-lantern lights give the space a calm, welcoming feel that works from breakfast to the evening.<br><br>Behind the calm interior sits a technically demanding operation: a full <strong>commercial kitchen, bakery and coffee bar</strong>, LPG systems and complete MEP infrastructure. Every piece of equipment needed power, water, drainage, exhaust and fresh air, coordinated with the kitchen consultant and the design.<br><br>The project went through <strong>Emaar, DDA and DEWA</strong> approvals and inspections, including the LPG installation and the kitchen ventilation. Our team prepared the technical drawings and submissions, answered every authority comment and coordinated testing and commissioning before handover.<br><br>Crystal Fit built the cafe <strong>from shell &amp; core</strong> to the client’s design – technical drawings, fit-out, MEP, joinery, authority submissions, testing and handover – and delivered a bakery-cafe that is as efficient behind the counter as it is beautiful in front of it. The arched niches, curved banquettes and terrazzo surfaces were made and finished to the exact lines of the design.<br><br>Before installation, the kitchen and bakery layout was verified on site against the equipment list, so that every connection – power, water, drainage, gas and exhaust – was in the right place when the equipment arrived. Finishes, fabrics and the terrazzo mix were approved on physical samples with the client before production, so the soft, natural palette of the design was kept from the drawings to the finished cafe.</p></div>
