<div class="pinfo__text"><p>A 360 sqm <a href="https://www.instagram.com/roasterscoffee_dxb/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">Roasters</a> <strong>standalone coffee house on Al Wasl Road, Jumeirah</strong>: a freestanding building on two levels with a rooftop terrace, a coffee bar on the ground floor and quieter seating above.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> interior design, technical and MEP drawings, approvals with <strong>Dubai Municipality, Dubai Civil Defence and DEWA</strong>, MEP and HVAC across both levels, the staircase with a backlit world map of coffee origins, rooftop terrace works and waterproofing, facade, signage and outdoor lighting, joinery and finishing works, project management to handover.<br><br><strong>The challenge was the whole building, not just a shop unit.</strong> A two-level cafe with a roof terrace adds work beyond a typical fit-out: the staircase, waterproofing of the terrace, outdoor lighting, and MEP running between the floors to serve the bar below and the seating above. We coordinated structure, services and interior on one set of drawings so that the building works as one cafe, with service routes that keep the kitchen and the bar efficient.<br><br>Because the cafe occupies a whole building, the project also covered the exterior: the white facade with brass lettering, signage and the roof terrace finishes, all specified to work in Dubai’s heat. Finishes were approved on samples, and each level was verified on site before joinery was produced. It was one of the early projects of our long partnership with Roasters, which has since grown to cafes in malls, communities and business districts across Dubai – a <strong>Food &amp; Beverage (F&amp;B) fit-out</strong> that started the relationship.</p></div>
