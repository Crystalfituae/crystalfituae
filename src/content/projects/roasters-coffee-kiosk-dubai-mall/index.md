<div class="pinfo__text"><p>A 70 sqm <a href="https://www.instagram.com/roasterscoffee_dxb/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">Roasters</a> <strong>specialty coffee kiosk in The Dubai Mall</strong>: an island on the mall floor in polished stainless steel, illuminated mesh panels and black metal, with a coffee bar and seating, seen from all sides.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> design development, technical and MEP drawings, <strong>Emaar and Dubai Mall approvals</strong>, bespoke metal and joinery fabrication in our workshop, MEP integrated into the base, specialist lighting, equipment installation, testing and project management to handover.<br><br><strong>The challenge was metalwork with built-in lighting.</strong> The kiosk is a precise piece of joinery and metalwork: stainless steel panels, illuminated mesh and black metal details meet without visible fixings, and the lighting is built into the structure so the kiosk glows without visible fittings. Every panel, joint and light line was developed in shop drawings, produced in the workshop and dry-assembled before delivery.<br><br>Most of the kiosk was fabricated off site as a kit of parts and assembled in the mall in short night shifts, which kept noisy and dusty work away from shoppers. Power, water and drainage were integrated into the base and coordinated with the mall services, and the position of the kiosk and its connections was verified on the mall floor before production. Every material – steel finish, mesh, stone and upholstery – was approved on samples. It is our second Roasters project in The Dubai Mall after the cafe island, and one of the most technically detailed pieces of fabrication we have produced for a <strong>Food &amp; Beverage (F&amp;B) fit-out</strong>.</p></div>
