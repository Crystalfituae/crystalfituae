<div class="pinfo__text"><p>A 70 sqm <a href="https://www.instagram.com/roasterscoffee_dxb/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">Roasters</a> <strong>specialty coffee kiosk in The Dubai Mall</strong>, designed as an architectural centrepiece on the mall floor. Like every island in the mall, it is seen from all sides, so each elevation was designed as a front.<br><br>Polished stainless steel, <strong>illuminated mesh panels</strong>, black metal details and natural stone tables give the kiosk a clean, modern identity, while the open layout connects the bar with a generous seating area. The lighting is built into the structure, so the kiosk glows without visible fittings.<br><br>Most of the kiosk was <strong>fabricated off site</strong> and assembled in the mall, which kept noisy and dusty work away from shoppers. All works were executed under Emaar and Dubai Mall requirements, with power, water and drainage integrated into the base and coordinated with the mall services.<br><br>Crystal Fit delivered the kiosk <strong>design &amp; build</strong> – design development, bespoke fabrication, MEP, specialist lighting, equipment installation, approvals, testing and handover. It is our second Roasters project in The Dubai Mall after the cafe island, and one of the most technically detailed pieces of joinery and metalwork we have produced for the brand.<br><br>Before fabrication, every material – steel finish, mesh, stone and upholstery – was approved on samples, and the position of the kiosk and its service connections was verified on the mall floor. The kit of parts was then produced in the workshop, which made the installation short, clean and precise and kept the time spent on the mall floor to a minimum.</p></div>
