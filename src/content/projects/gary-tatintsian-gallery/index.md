<div class="pinfo__text"><p><a href="https://tatintsian.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">Gary Tatintsian Gallery</a> is a 220 sqm <strong>contemporary art gallery in Dubai Design District (d3)</strong>: a minimalist, flexible exhibition space made of two joined units, for international artists and high-profile visitors.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> full technical review of the existing works, rebuilding of architectural and MEP elements, joining two units into one gallery, approvals with <strong>TECOM, DDA, Dubai Civil Defence and DEWA</strong>, seamless cementitious flooring, fire-rated decorative wall panels, joinery, technical supervision and project management to final approvals. The interior design was by the client’s designer.<br><br><strong>The challenge was taking over a failed project.</strong> Several previous contractors had been unable to complete the gallery or obtain approvals. We started with a full technical review of what was built and what was documented, identified the elements that would never pass inspection, and rebuilt the critical architectural and MEP parts instead of patching them. All drawings and submissions were redone and coordinated into one set that matched the site.<br><br>Joining two separate units into one gallery meant changes to walls, fire separation, services and DEWA connections, all approved by DDA and Civil Defence. The finishes had to stay calm and gallery-grade: seamless cementitious flooring without joints, fire-rated decorative wall panels that still read as clean white walls, and lighting suitable for changing exhibitions. The project was completed with final approvals from DDA, DCD, DEWA and TECOM – proof that we can recover a project others could not finish. The client received a complete, consistent set of as-built drawings – something the gallery had never had before.</p></div>
