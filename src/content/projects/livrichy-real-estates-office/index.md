<div class="pinfo__text"><p><a href="https://livrichy.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">Livrichy Real Estate</a> is a 270 sqm <strong>real estate office in Park Square Tower, Dubai Hills</strong>, within the Emaar community: reception and client lounge, a library, meeting and Zoom rooms, a boardroom, CEO and GM offices and an open workspace.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> interior design, technical and MEP drawings, approvals with <strong>Emaar, Dubai Municipality, Dubai Civil Defence and DEWA</strong>, MEP and HVAC, partitions and glazing, bespoke reception counters and joinery, layered lighting, finishing works and project management – <strong>turnkey</strong>, through to handover.<br><br><strong>The challenge was fitting nine different functions into 270 sqm.</strong> The office had to impress clients at the front and work for daily sales at the back. We zoned the plan so that the reception, lounge, library and boardroom form the client route, while the open workspace and management offices sit behind it. Meeting and Zoom rooms were given acoustic partitions and their own air and lighting control, so calls and negotiations stay private next to a busy sales floor.<br><br>Working in an operating Emaar office tower meant landlord approval of every drawing, works within building hours and protection of common areas. HVAC, sprinklers and lighting were re-planned around the new partitions and approved by Emaar and Civil Defence. The bespoke reception counters and joinery were made in the workshop and installed in a short programme. Premium finishes and layered lighting give the office a hospitality feel while keeping it practical – a typical <strong>office fit-out</strong> by our team in Dubai. The client received as-built drawings and completion certificates with the keys.</p></div>
