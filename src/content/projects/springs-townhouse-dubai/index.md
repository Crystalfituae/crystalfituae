<div class="pinfo__text"><p>A full refresh of a 200 sqm <strong>family townhouse in The Springs</strong>, Dubai – inside and out – turning a standard community home into a light, calm house with a private pool garden.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> interior design, technical and MEP drawings, community approvals, MEP modifications, wall panelling and mouldings, oak-look herringbone flooring, a rebuilt kitchen, all bathrooms redone, joinery, the garden with a plunge pool, timber-look deck and outdoor dining terrace, and project management – design and fit-out, through to handover.<br><br><strong>The challenge was rebuilding everything while keeping to the community rules.</strong> The Springs is an established community with rules for external works, pools and working hours. We obtained the community approvals for the pool and garden works before starting, and planned the programme so that indoor and outdoor works ran in parallel without blocking access to the house.<br><br>Inside, every bathroom was stripped back, re-waterproofed and rebuilt – from stone-look tiles and a round-window shower to a dark terrazzo powder room – with drainage and water points relocated where the new layouts required it. The kitchen was rebuilt with handle-less grey cabinetry and a patterned splashback, and classic panelling and mouldings were set out to align with doors and windows. Outside, the plunge pool was built with its waterproofing, filtration and lighting, and the deck and dining terrace were laid around it with proper falls for drainage. The townhouse was handed over ready for family life – an example of our <strong>villa and townhouse renovation</strong> work in Dubai.</p></div>
