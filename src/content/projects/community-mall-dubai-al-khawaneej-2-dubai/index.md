<div class="pinfo__text"><p>An <strong>architectural concept for a G+2+roof community mall</strong> of 13,500 sqm in Al Khawaneej 2, Dubai, serving the surrounding residential neighbourhood with shops, services and food outlets.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> architectural concept design – site planning, massing studies, floor layouts, facades, the entrance canopy, landscaping and parking concept, and 3D visualisation – with project management of the design stage. Detailed design and construction were by others.<br><br><strong>The challenge was to make an efficient retail box feel like a destination.</strong> A community mall has to be economical to build and easy to lease: simple floor plates, flexible shop units, short service routes and enough parking. Instead of adding cost to the whole building, we concentrated the architecture in a few elements – a striking entrance canopy of diagonal steel trusses, large shopfront glazing and clean stone-look facades – and kept the main structure rational and repetitive.<br><br>The site plan separates cars from pedestrians: landscaped parking with shaded walkways, a pedestrian bridge and drop-off at the entrance, with service and delivery access kept to the back of the building. Shaded outdoor areas at ground level give food outlets space for seating, and the roof level is planned for future use. Every option was presented to the client in 3D, so massing, facades and costs could be compared before the concept was frozen. The result is a human-scale neighbourhood destination that remains simple and cost-efficient to build – an example of our architectural design work beyond interiors. The concept package was handed to the client for the next design stages.</p></div>
