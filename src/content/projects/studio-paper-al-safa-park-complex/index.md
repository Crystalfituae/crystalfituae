<div class="pinfo__text"><p><a href="https://www.instagram.com/studiopaper.ae/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">Studio Paper</a> is a 120 sqm <strong>print and design studio with a brew bar in Al Safa Park Complex</strong>, Dubai: a shop for art prints and posters combined with a coffee counter and seating.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> interior design, display joinery for rotating poster collections, the cafe counter built to the brand’s equipment list, herringbone timber flooring, lighting, graphic signage, finishing works and project management to handover.<br><br><strong>The challenge was combining retail and Food &amp; Beverage (F&amp;B) in one space.</strong> Customers browsing the walls and guests ordering at the counter have two different flows, and both had to stay comfortable. The layout keeps clear sightlines from the entrance to the counter and enough open floor to move around the displays, and the counter sits inside the shop rather than dividing it.<br><br>The display system was the key piece of joinery. It was mocked up with the owners before production so that poster sizes, spacing, hanging system and lighting suited the way they change the collection, and the prints can be rearranged without tools or damage to the walls. Lighting was designed for both uses – even, gallery-style light on the prints and warmer light around the coffee bar. Finishes and fabrics were approved on samples before ordering. The result works as a shop, a gallery and a neighbourhood cafe at the same time – proof that a well-planned small unit can carry several businesses under one roof. The counter was built to the brand’s equipment list and tested with the machines before opening.</p></div>
