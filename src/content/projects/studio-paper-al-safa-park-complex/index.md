<div class="pinfo__text"><p><strong><a href="https://www.instagram.com/studiopaper.ae/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">Studio Paper</a></strong> is a 120 sqm <strong>print and design studio with a brew bar</strong> in Al Safa Park Complex, Dubai. Visitors come to browse and buy art prints and posters – and stay for coffee and cake.<br><br>Herringbone timber floors, white walls for rotating poster displays, a playful tiger rug and graphic signage create a creative, gallery-like setting. The display joinery lets the team change the prints easily, while the cafe counter sits naturally within the shop rather than dividing it.<br><br>Combining retail and <strong>Food &amp; Beverage (F&amp;B)</strong> in one space means balancing two different flows: customers browsing the walls and guests ordering at the counter. The layout keeps both comfortable, with clear sightlines from the entrance to the counter and enough open floor to move around the displays. Lighting was planned for both uses – even, gallery-style light on the prints and warmer light around the coffee bar.<br><br>Crystal Fit delivered the project as <strong>design and build</strong> – interior design, display joinery, the cafe counter, flooring, lighting and finishing works. The result is a space that works as a shop, a gallery and a neighbourhood cafe at the same time, and a reminder that a well-planned small unit can carry several businesses under one roof.<br><br>As in every project, finishes and fabrics were approved on samples before ordering, and the display system was mocked up with the owners so the poster sizes, spacing and lighting suited the way they work. The cafe counter was built to the brand’s equipment list, so the brew bar worked from the first day of trading.</p></div>
