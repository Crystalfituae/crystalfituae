<div class="pinfo__text"><p>A 70 sqm <strong>retail store for the cookware brand <a href="https://fissman.ae/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">Fissman</a></strong> in Circle Mall, Jumeirah Village Circle, Dubai, with a fully glazed shopfront, timber wall shelving, freestanding display tables and a green ceiling feature.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> interior design, technical and MEP drawings, landlord and authority approvals, MEP and lighting works, bespoke shelving and display joinery, the ceiling feature, finishing works, technical supervision and project management – design and build, through to handover.<br><br><strong>The challenge was displaying hundreds of products in 70 sqm.</strong> Cookware comes in many sizes – from spoons to large pots – and the store had to show the full range without looking crowded. We planned the shelving as a system: adjustable shelf heights on a fixed module, integrated lighting in every bay so products are lit from the front without shadows, hidden stock storage in the base of the wall units and freestanding tables that can be moved for promotions.<br><br>The joinery was designed for loads – shelves for heavy cast-iron and steel products were specified and fixed accordingly, and the wall units were anchored to take the weight. The shopfront glazing, signage and ceiling feature were approved by the mall before fabrication. All joinery was made in the workshop and installed in a short programme with the lighting tested on site, so the brand could stock the shelves and open quickly. A compact <strong>retail fit-out</strong> where the store works as the brand’s best display. Mall inspections and the final handover were closed without comments, and the client received as-built drawings.</p></div>
