<div class="pinfo__text"><p>Villa Moderna is a 2,170 sqm <strong>private residence in Nad Al Sheba</strong>, Dubai – a sculptural composition of stone-clad volumes, deep cantilevers and wide glazing.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> <strong>architectural design</strong> and <strong>technical supervision</strong> of the construction from 2015 to 2019 – concept and detailed design, facade and stone-cladding details, review of the contractor’s shop drawings and materials, regular site inspections and design-stage project management. Construction was carried out by the client’s contractor.<br><br><strong>The challenge was building the cantilevers and stone cladding exactly as designed.</strong> The bold cantilevered forms and precise stone cladding left no room for site improvisation. For four years our team checked the contractor’s shop drawings against the design, verified the setting-out of the cladding module, inspected the structure and the stone fixings before closing, and resolved conflicts between structure, services and facade on site before they became defects.<br><br>Feature elements such as the backlit natural stone walls were developed with mock-ups and checked on site with the lighting on, until the stone colour, thickness and light gave the intended result. Wide glazing and the cantilevers were coordinated so that the openings stay frameless where the design required it. Long technical supervision of a private residence of this size shows how we protect the design through construction: the house was completed as a coherent piece of architecture, with the stone volumes and cantilevers built as drawn – our largest <strong>private villa design</strong> project in Dubai. Every inspection was recorded in site reports for the client, so decisions and corrections were traceable from start to finish.</p></div>
