<div class="pinfo__text"><p><a href="https://amvclinic-eng.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">AMV Clinic</a> is a 180 sqm <strong>premium aesthetic clinic in Bay Square, Business Bay</strong>, managed by Dubai Holding: a bespoke reception and waiting area, treatment rooms, luxury washrooms and back-of-house areas.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> interior design, technical and MEP drawings, approvals with <strong>Dubai Holding, Dubai Health Authority (DHA), Dubai Municipality, Dubai Civil Defence and DEWA</strong>, MEP works, custom joinery, marble-look porcelain, decorative mouldings, integrated lighting, finishing works and project management – <strong>turnkey</strong>, through to handover.<br><br><strong>The challenge was the medical approval route.</strong> A clinic has to meet DHA facility requirements before it can be licensed: room sizes, patient flow, hand-wash points, hygienic and cleanable finishes, ventilation and the services that DHA inspectors check on site. We designed the layout to those requirements from the first drawing, prepared the DHA submissions, answered every comment and attended the inspections until the facility was approved – in parallel with Dubai Holding’s own landlord reviews and inspections.<br><br>Inside, the premium look had to work with clinical rules. Mouldings, joinery and lighting were detailed with sealed, wipe-clean surfaces and no dust traps, washroom and treatment-room finishes were selected for daily disinfection, and MEP points for medical equipment were coordinated with the clinic’s equipment list before the walls were closed. Finishes were approved on samples and every dimension was verified on site. The clinic was handed over ready for its DHA licence – an example of our <strong>commercial fit-out</strong> work for healthcare in Dubai. The client received as-built drawings and all completion certificates with the keys.</p></div>
