<div class="pinfo__text"><p>A 620 sqm <strong>contemporary villa interior in Al Khawaneej</strong>, Dubai: a double-height entrance hall, living and dining areas, family rooms, bedrooms and bathrooms.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> complete <strong>interior design</strong> – space planning and layouts, concept, 3D visualisation, lighting design, materials, finishes and furniture specifications – with design-stage project management. Construction was carried out by the client’s contractor.<br><br><strong>The challenge was the double-height entrance hall.</strong> The hall sets the tone for the whole house, and its key elements – a floating stone staircase and a cascading chandelier – are as much engineering as design. We designed the staircase with a hidden steel structure inside the stone treads, so that each step appears to float from the wall, and worked out the chandelier’s height, fixing and maintenance access so that it reads from both the ground floor and the gallery above. Both elements were documented in detailed drawings for the contractor.<br><br>The rest of the villa follows a calm, modern language. Living areas combine light stone walls, timber ceilings and open shelving; bathrooms and bedrooms rely on concrete-look surfaces, warm wood and soft lighting. Every room was presented to the client in 3D before the specifications were issued, so materials, lighting and furniture were agreed on screen rather than changed on site. The specification package listed every finish, fitting and furniture item with references and quantities, which allowed the client to tender and build the interior exactly as designed – an example of our <strong>villa interior design</strong> work in Dubai.</p></div>
