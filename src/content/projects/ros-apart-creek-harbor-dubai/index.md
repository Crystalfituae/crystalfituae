<div class="pinfo__text"><p>A 98 sqm <strong>family apartment in Creek Rise Tower 2, Dubai Creek Harbour</strong>: living and dining room, kitchen, a compact study corner, a master bedroom and a kids’ room.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> interior design, technical and MEP drawings, building management approvals, MEP modifications, all built-in furniture made to measure in oak – TV wall, kitchen, study corner and wardrobes – the marble feature wall, the kids’ room wardrobe and desk, lighting, finishing works and project management – design and fit-out, through to handover.<br><br><strong>The challenge was making 98 sqm work for a whole family.</strong> Every metre had to carry storage without the apartment feeling full. We designed the oak joinery as one continuous system that runs from the TV wall to the kitchen and the study corner, with hidden storage, integrated lighting and cable routes. In the kids’ room, the pink wardrobe and integrated desk give a child a place to sleep, study and play in a small footprint.<br><br>All joinery was measured on site after the walls were prepared, produced in the workshop and installed in a short programme, with joints and handles aligned across the whole apartment. The marble feature wall and circular pendant define the living room, and lighting was planned with the joinery so that fixtures, sockets and switches sit exactly where the furniture needs them. Works followed the tower’s rules on access, noise and deliveries, with approvals from the building management obtained before starting. The result is a warm, practical family home – an example of our <strong>apartment design and fit-out</strong> work in Dubai.</p></div>
