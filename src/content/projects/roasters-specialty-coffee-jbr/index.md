<div class="pinfo__text"><p>A 280 sqm <a href="https://www.instagram.com/roasterscoffee_dxb/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">Roasters</a> <strong>specialty coffee house in Amwaj, JBR</strong>, with an illuminated arched facade facing the beachfront. The location sees heavy traffic from residents and tourists, so the cafe had to stand out on the street and handle busy service from morning to night.<br><br>The double-height interior is zoned into lounge areas with terracotta and green velvet seating, marble tables, media walls and planting. A timber-slatted ceiling feature and warm lighting bring the large volume down to a comfortable scale, while the coffee bar stays visible from every seat.<br><br>The <strong>facade lighting</strong> was designed and installed as part of the fit-out, turning the arched shopfront into a landmark along the walk at night. Inside, the double height required careful planning of air conditioning, lighting and maintenance access, so that services stay hidden and easy to reach. Hard-wearing finishes were chosen for a beachfront cafe, where sand, humidity and heavy footfall are part of daily life.<br><br>Crystal Fit delivered the cafe as <strong>design and build</strong> – interior design, approvals with <strong>Dubai Municipality, Dubai Civil Defence and DEWA</strong>, MEP and HVAC, facade lighting, bespoke joinery and finishing works – through to handover with all completion certificates.<br><br>The project followed our usual sequence for Roasters: a detailed BOQ and one agreement, approval of the velvet, marble and timber on samples, on-site verification of the double-height shell, then approvals and works running in parallel. That preparation is what made it possible to build a large, detailed cafe on a busy beachfront without delays or surprises, and to open on the date planned with the brand.</p></div>
