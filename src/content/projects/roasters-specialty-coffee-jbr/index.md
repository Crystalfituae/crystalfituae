<div class="pinfo__text"><p>A 280 sqm <a href="https://www.instagram.com/roasterscoffee_dxb/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">Roasters</a> <strong>specialty coffee house in Amwaj, JBR</strong>: a double-height interior with lounge zones, media walls, a timber-slatted ceiling feature and an illuminated arched facade facing the beachfront.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> interior design, technical and MEP drawings, approvals with <strong>Dubai Municipality, Dubai Civil Defence and DEWA</strong>, MEP and HVAC, facade lighting design and installation, the timber-slatted ceiling, bespoke joinery, finishing works and project management to handover with completion certificates.<br><br><strong>The challenge was the double height and the facade.</strong> The large volume needed air conditioning sized for its full height, lighting and the slatted ceiling feature installed at height, and maintenance access to services that stay hidden from guests. The arched shopfront lighting was designed and installed as part of the fit-out, with fittings, drivers and cabling concealed in the facade so the arches glow without visible equipment and can be serviced from inside.<br><br>A beachfront cafe lives with sand, humidity and heavy footfall, so floor finishes, upholstery and joinery were selected and detailed for that environment. The project followed our usual sequence for Roasters: a detailed BOQ and one agreement, approval of the velvet, marble and timber on samples, on-site verification of the double-height shell, then approvals and works in parallel. That preparation is what made it possible to hand over on schedule a cafe that has become a landmark on the JBR walk at night – a typical <strong>Food &amp; Beverage (F&amp;B) fit-out</strong> by our team. Facade lighting was tested at night before handover.</p></div>
