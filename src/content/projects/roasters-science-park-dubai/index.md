<div class="pinfo__text"><p>A 566 sqm <a href="https://www.instagram.com/roasterscoffee_dxb/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">Roasters</a> <strong>specialty coffee house in Dubai Science Park</strong>: a double-height hall with an exposed industrial ceiling, a long travertine bar, lounge zones, an art wall and a mezzanine, serving the offices of the business park.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> interior design and 3D visuals, technical and MEP drawings, landlord and authority approvals, HVAC, power and lighting design and installation, the travertine bar counter with a brushed-steel back wall, illuminated signage, black slatted panels, custom joinery, the mezzanine finishes and project management to handover.<br><br><strong>The challenge was the double-height volume.</strong> A large open hall needs air conditioning and fresh air sized for its full volume, lighting at height, and access for installation and future maintenance – all with an exposed ceiling, where every duct, cable tray and track light stays visible. Our engineers designed HVAC, power and linear track lighting around the open ceiling, set out the routes in one coordinated drawing and installed them from scaffolding before the floor finishes, so the exposed look stays clean and ordered.<br><br>The travertine bar, steel back wall and slatted panels concealing the service areas were detailed in shop drawings and approved on samples together with the leather and timber finishes. Work at height, MEP and joinery were sequenced so that no trade damaged the work of another. The cafe was handed over ready to trade and continues our long partnership with Roasters across Dubai. Testing and commissioning of every system was completed before handover, and the client received as-built drawings and completion certificates.</p></div>
