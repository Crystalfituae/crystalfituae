<div class="pinfo__text"><p>A complete <strong>luxury renovation of a 4-bedroom apartment on Bluewaters</strong>, Dubai – 205 sqm taken back to shell &amp; core and rebuilt as a fully customised home.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> building management and <strong>Meraas</strong> approvals, authority approvals, full demolition, shell &amp; core preparation, MEP modifications, bespoke joinery, premium natural stone, decorative finishes, coordinated lighting, technical coordination and project management – the full fit-out through to handover. The interior design was by the client’s designer.<br><br><strong>The challenge was the material junctions.</strong> Premium natural stone, bespoke joinery, decorative finishes and lighting meet everywhere in this apartment – at floors, walls, ceilings, doors and built-in furniture. We developed every junction in shop drawings with the designer before production, set out the stone slab by slab, and sequenced the trades so that each surface was finished and protected before the next one met it. Nothing was left to be “solved on site”.<br><br>The project covered the whole lifecycle of a renovation in a managed island community: building management and Meraas approvals before any work, a full demolition with debris removal under strict rules, preparation of the shell, and MEP modifications coordinated with the building systems and inspected before closing walls and ceilings. Lighting was tested with the designer at night before handover. The apartment was handed over as a finished home in which every detail matches the design – an example of our <strong>luxury residential fit-out</strong> for designer-led projects in Dubai. The client received as-built drawings and all approvals closed with the keys.</p></div>
