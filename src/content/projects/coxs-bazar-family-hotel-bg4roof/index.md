<div class="pinfo__text"><p>An <strong>architectural concept for a 5-star family resort</strong> of 8,500 sqm (B+G+4+roof) on the coast of Cox’s Bazar, Bangladesh, with rooms, restaurants, roof pools and gardens, play areas and outdoor dining.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> architectural concept design – massing studies, the amenities programme, floor layouts, facades, roof and landscape concept and 3D visualisation – with project management of the design stage. Detailed design and construction were by others.<br><br><strong>The challenge was the climate.</strong> Cox’s Bazar is hot, humid and exposed to the monsoon, so the building had to stay comfortable with as little mechanical cooling as possible. We carved the building mass to create cross ventilation through the corridors and public areas, used deep terraced balconies with soft curves to shade the facades, and oriented the rooms so that every one of them faces the water while avoiding the worst of the afternoon sun.<br><br>The amenities programme was planned for families: restaurants and outdoor dining at ground level, play areas away from the quiet rooms, and roof pools and gardens that turn the top of the building into a second leisure level. Service routes, kitchens and back-of-house were placed in the basement and at the back of the plot, so guests and staff never cross. Each massing option was tested in 3D for views, shading and the number of keys before the client chose the final concept. The project shows our architectural design work for hospitality outside the UAE. The concept package was handed over to the local design team for the next stages.</p></div>
