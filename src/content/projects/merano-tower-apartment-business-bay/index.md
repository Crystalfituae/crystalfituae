<div class="pinfo__text"><p>An 85 sqm <strong>apartment in Merano Tower, Business Bay</strong>, refreshed into a calm, hotel-like home with a boutique-style bedroom and a light, minimal living room.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> interior design, technical drawings, building management approvals, MEP modifications, grey fluted wall panels, the arched mirror niche, upholstery, timber flooring, joinery, lighting, curtains and project management – design and fit-out, through to handover.<br><br><strong>The challenge was a fast refresh of an occupied tower apartment.</strong> The client wanted a new look without a long renovation. We kept the existing layout and services where possible and concentrated the change in finishes, panelling, lighting and soft furnishings, where the visible effect is greatest. All works were approved by the building management in advance and carried out within the tower’s permitted hours, with lifts and corridors protected.<br><br>The bedroom’s grey fluted wall panels and arched mirror niche were made in the workshop to dimensions verified on site, with the lighting integrated into the niche, and installed in a short programme. The timber floor was laid over the existing slab with an acoustic underlay to meet the building’s rules on impact noise. In the living room, soft curves, full-height curtains and minimal furniture keep the small space light. The apartment was handed over ready to live in after a short programme – an example of how our <strong>apartment fit-out</strong> team in Dubai can transform a home quickly by focusing on the details that matter most. Final inspection by the building management was closed without comments.</p></div>
