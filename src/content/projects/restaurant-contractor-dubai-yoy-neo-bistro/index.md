<div class="pinfo__text"><p>The relocation of the <a href="https://www.instagram.com/yoy_dubai/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">YOY</a> Ukrainian fire-cooking restaurant into a 350 sqm <strong>fine-dining venue at Shangri-La Hotel, Sheikh Zayed Road</strong>, Dubai, with bespoke ceiling decor and lighting by Ukrainian designer Mykola Kabluka.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> interior design development, approvals within the hotel and with the authorities, joinery, finishes, lighting, coordination and installation of the decor, value engineering and project management to handover.<br><br><strong>The challenge was a short deadline in an operating five-star hotel.</strong> To deliver on time, the existing kitchen, fire and life safety and electrical systems of the unit were kept with minor adjustments, avoiding major re-inspections. The transformation focused on materials, colours and lighting – where the biggest change for guests could be made with the least disruption to approved systems. Works followed the hotel’s strict rules on access, noise and deliveries and were coordinated daily with the hotel management.<br><br>The sculptural ceiling installations were planned with the designer: fixing points, loads, lighting connections and sprinkler clearances were set out before installation, so the decor floats above the dining room without conflict with the ceiling services. Value engineering was applied across the scope to keep the budget under control while keeping YOY’s signature elements. The project continued our work with YOY after the original restaurant at The Pointe, Palm Jumeirah – a <strong>Food &amp; Beverage (F&amp;B) fit-out</strong> delivered inside a live hotel. The decor installation was completed at night in agreed phases, so the hotel corridors and neighbouring outlets were never disrupted, and the restaurant opened on the planned date with every ceiling sculpture lit and in place.</p></div>
