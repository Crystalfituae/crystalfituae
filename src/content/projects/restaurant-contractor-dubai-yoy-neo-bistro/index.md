<div class="pinfo__text"><p>The relocation of the <a href="https://www.instagram.com/yoy_dubai/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">YOY</a> Ukrainian fire-cooking restaurant into a 350 sqm <strong>fine-dining venue at Shangri-La Hotel, Sheikh Zayed Road</strong>, Dubai, with bespoke ceiling decor and lighting by Ukrainian designer Mykola Kabluka.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> <strong>interior design</strong> and design development, technical drawings, material and lighting specifications, value engineering and <strong>technical consultancy</strong> to the client during construction. The fit-out works and the installation of the decor were carried out by the client’s contractors.<br><br><strong>The challenge was a new restaurant on a short deadline without new approvals.</strong> The unit inside the operating five-star hotel already had an approved kitchen, fire and life safety and electrical systems. Our design kept these systems with minor adjustments, avoiding major re-inspections, and moved the transformation into materials, colours and lighting – where the biggest change for guests could be made with the least disruption to approved services. Every design decision was checked against what the existing ceiling, sprinklers and power could take.<br><br>We worked with the decor designer on the integration of the sculptural ceiling installations: zones, heights, lighting connections and clearances to the ceiling services were set out in our drawings, so the contractors could install the decor without conflicts. During construction our team answered the contractors’ technical questions, reviewed proposed alternatives against the design and the budget, and advised the client on value engineering. The project continued our work with YOY after the original restaurant at The Pointe, Palm Jumeirah, which we built – here our role was the design and the technical guidance, while the build was by others.</p></div>
