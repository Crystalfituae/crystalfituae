<div class="pinfo__text"><p><a href="https://www.inthepink.ae/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">In the Pink</a> is a 280 sqm <strong>wellness venue with a healthy cafe in Dubai Hills</strong>, within the Emaar community: two stretching studios (one with heating lamps), a massage room, premium changing rooms, reception, retail display, a cafe and a full kitchen.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> interior design, technical and MEP drawings, approvals with <strong>Emaar, Dubai Municipality, Dubai Civil Defence and DEWA</strong>, shell &amp; core fit-out, MEP including a fresh air handling unit, kitchen services, hand-applied microcement finishes, custom joinery and project management – design and build, through to handover.<br><br><strong>The challenge was Emaar inspections against an opening date.</strong> Emaar’s construction standards meant <strong>more than 50 inspections</strong> before the NOC and handover, while the client faced penalties for a late opening. We ran the approvals as a project of their own: every inspection was planned in the programme, prepared in advance and attended by our team, and comments were closed the same week, so the venue opened without penalties.<br><br>Technically the venue combines very different uses under one roof. The heated studio needed its own power, controls and air balance; the changing rooms and wet areas needed waterproofing and extraction; the kitchen needed exhaust and fresh air; and a dedicated fresh air handling unit was installed to keep air quality right for exercise. The hand-applied microcement was laid continuously across floors and walls, and the joinery was made to fit it. The result is a calm, practical space – a mixed-use <strong>commercial fit-out</strong> from shell &amp; core. As-built drawings were handed over with the keys.</p></div>
