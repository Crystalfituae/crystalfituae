<div class="pinfo__text"><p><a href="https://www.instagram.com/muhamusic/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">Muha Music &amp; Fine Arts</a> is a 450 sqm <strong>music and fine arts school on the podium level of JBR</strong>, Dubai: classrooms, a sound recording studio and booth, two dance halls, a gym, offices, pantry and washrooms.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> interior design, technical and MEP drawings, approvals with <strong>Dubai Municipality, Dubai Civil Defence and DEWA</strong>, acoustic design and construction, MEP works, joinery, murals and colour-coded doors, finishing works and project management – <strong>turnkey</strong>, through to handover.<br><br><strong>The challenge was acoustic separation.</strong> A drum lesson, a piano class, a dance hall and a recording studio work side by side, and sound must not travel between them. We designed dedicated wall and ceiling build-ups for each room type, with the highest separation around the recording studio and booth, sealed every service penetration and coordinated ducts and doors so that they did not become sound paths. Room by room, the acoustic details were drawn, built and checked before finishes went on.<br><br>The dance halls and gym needed robust floors, mirrors and ventilation for physical activity, while the classrooms needed good daylight and a calm background for teaching. On top of the technical layer, bold murals, colour-coded doors and playful furniture make the school easy to navigate and inspiring for children. Approvals and inspections were completed in parallel with the works, and the school opened on schedule – an example of how our <strong>commercial fit-out</strong> team handles technically demanding education spaces. Every acoustic room was tested with sound before handover, and the client received as-built drawings and completion certificates.</p></div>
