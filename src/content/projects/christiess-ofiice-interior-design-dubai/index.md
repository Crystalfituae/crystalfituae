<div class="pinfo__text"><p>An art gallery and office for <strong><a href="https://www.christies.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">Christie's</a> auction house</strong> in Gate Village, DIFC – a minimal, gallery-grade space for exhibitions, private viewings and client meetings.<br><br>The design keeps the architecture quiet so that the objects speak: neutral walls, polished concrete-look floors, a sculptural reception desk and flexible track lighting for changing displays of watches, jewellery and fine art. Works were coordinated under DIFC requirements in a fully operational district.<br><br>Crystal Fit delivered the <strong>design and fit-out</strong> of the gallery and offices, including approvals and lighting.</p></div>
