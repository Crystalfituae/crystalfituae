<div class="pinfo__text"><p>Renovation of the <strong>lobby and all-day dining restaurant of Mercure Gold Hotel</strong> on Al Mina Road, Dubai – 1,670 sqm of public areas in an operating hotel.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> interior design, technical and MEP drawings, approvals with <strong>Dubai Municipality and Dubai Civil Defence</strong>, MEP works, sculptural column cladding in timber and brass finish, laser-cut screens, bespoke joinery, flooring, lighting, finishing works and project management – design and fit-out of the public areas, through to handover.<br><br><strong>The challenge was renovating while the hotel stayed open.</strong> The lobby is the entrance for every guest, and the restaurant serves breakfast, lunch and dinner. We split the works into phases, kept a protected route from the entrance to reception and the lifts at all times, and scheduled noisy and dusty works for agreed hours, so the hotel never had to close its public areas.<br><br>The design turns a problem into the main feature: the existing structural columns in the lobby and restaurant could not be removed, so we clad them as sculptural timber and brass-finish elements that now define the space. Laser-cut screens divide the restaurant into intimate zones without blocking light, and onyx-look floors, blue upholstery and pendant lighting give the hotel a fresh, recognisable identity. Column cladding and screens were made in the workshop and installed in short phases. The renovation gave the hotel a new public face without closing it – an example of our <strong>hotel fit-out</strong> work. Each phase was handed over to the hotel operations team as soon as it was complete, with Civil Defence inspections passed for every area.</p></div>
