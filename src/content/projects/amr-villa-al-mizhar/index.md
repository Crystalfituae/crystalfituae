<div class="pinfo__text"><p>A 440 sqm <strong>neo-classic villa interior in Al Mizhar</strong>, Dubai, designed for a large family: a grand entrance hall, living and dining rooms, bedrooms for parents and children and a set of marble bathrooms.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> complete <strong>interior design</strong> – layouts, concept, photorealistic 3D visualisation, lighting design, finishes and furniture specification – with design-stage project management. Construction was carried out by the client’s contractor.<br><br><strong>The challenge was planning for a large family in a classical style.</strong> Neo-classic interiors depend on symmetry and proportion, while a large family needs many bedrooms, bathrooms and storage spaces. We reworked the layouts so that the formal rooms keep their axes and symmetry, while private rooms gained wardrobes, storage and bathrooms without breaking the classical composition. The children’s rooms take a lighter, playful direction within the same language.<br><br>Moulded ceilings and polished marble floors with inlaid patterns form a calm backdrop. Every ceiling was drawn with its mouldings, light coves, chandelier positions and AC grilles coordinated, and every floor inlay was drawn to scale for cutting. Statement chandeliers, velvet upholstery and deep plum accents were specified with references and quantities, so the client could purchase and install them exactly as visualised. The photorealistic 3D for every room let the family agree on colours, fabrics and furniture before any order was placed. The result is a complete package for building a warm, classic family home – an example of our <strong>villa interior design</strong> work in Dubai. Lighting scenes for every room were included in the package for the contractor.</p></div>
