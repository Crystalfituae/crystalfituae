<div class="pinfo__text"><p>A 77 sqm <a href="https://www.instagram.com/roasterscoffee_dxb/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">Roasters</a> <strong>open cafe island inside The Dubai Mall</strong>: a stone-clad bar around a black totem with the brand logo, a pastry display and seating, visible from every direction on the mall floor.<br><br><strong>Our scope of works:</strong> interior design and design development, technical and MEP drawings, <strong>Emaar and Dubai Mall approvals</strong>, MEP works, stone cladding, bespoke joinery and metalwork made in our workshop, finishing works and project management to handover.<br><br><strong>The challenge was an island with no back-of-house.</strong> With no walls and no storage room, every side of the island had to look finished and work for service at the same time. Storage, equipment, power, water and drainage were integrated into the joinery and the floor and coordinated with the mall services. The Dubai Mall has strict design and fire-safety rules and limits works on the mall floor, so the island was fabricated off site as a precise kit of parts and installed in short night shifts with minimal disruption to shoppers.<br><br>Every material – stone, metal, upholstery – was approved on samples and checked against the mall’s fire and finish standards before fabrication. The position of the island and its service connections was verified on the mall floor before production, so the kit fitted first time and the time on site stayed short. The cafe was handed over ready to serve one of the busiest malls in the world and was followed by our second Roasters project there, the specialty coffee kiosk – an example of <strong>Food &amp; Beverage (F&amp;B) fit-out</strong> under the strictest landlord rules.</p></div>
