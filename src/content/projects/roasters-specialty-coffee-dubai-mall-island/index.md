<div class="pinfo__text"><p>A 77 sqm <a href="https://www.instagram.com/roasterscoffee_dxb/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">Roasters</a> <strong>open cafe island inside The Dubai Mall</strong>, visible from every direction. With no walls to hide behind, every side of the island had to look finished and work for service at the same time.<br><br>A sculptural black totem with the brand logo anchors the island and makes it easy to find in the mall. Around it, the stone-clad bar, a pastry display and grey upholstered seating form a clean, compact layout that works in one of the busiest malls in the world.<br><br>An island has no back-of-house, so storage, equipment, power, water and drainage were integrated into the joinery and the floor, coordinated with the mall services. The Dubai Mall has strict design and safety rules and limits works on the mall floor, so fabrication was done off site in our workshop and the installation was planned in short shifts with minimal disruption to shoppers.<br><br>Crystal Fit delivered the island as <strong>design and build</strong> under <strong>Emaar and Dubai Mall</strong> requirements – design development, approvals, MEP, bespoke joinery, stone cladding and finishing – and handed over a cafe ready to serve the mall’s crowds. It was followed by a second Roasters project in the mall, the specialty coffee kiosk.<br><br>Every material on the island – stone, metal, upholstery – was approved on samples and checked against the mall’s fire and finish standards before fabrication. The pieces were then assembled on the mall floor like a precise kit, which kept the time on site short and the quality under our control from the workshop to the final inspection.</p></div>
